The Hills District Lawn Care Team That Turns Up

Green With Envy Lawn & Property Maintenance is a locally owned lawn care business based in Kellyville Ridge, right in the heart of Sydney's Hills District. We provide professional lawn mowing in Kellyville and the surrounding suburbs, along with hedge trimming, garden maintenance, weed control and complete property upkeep for both residential and commercial customers.

Because we live and work in the Hills, we know exactly what local lawns go through — the buffalo and kikuyu that surge after summer rain, the shaded strips along new estates in The Ponds and Box Hill, and the established gardens of Castle Hill and Baulkham Hills that need a more careful hand. Every property gets a service plan matched to its grass type, growth rate and season, not a one-size-fits-all pass with the mower.

Whether you need a regular fortnightly cut, a one-off tidy before an inspection, or full-service grounds care for a commercial site, we show up when we say we will, do the job properly, and leave your place looking sharp — edges crisp, clippings gone, gates closed.
